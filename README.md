## Learning.md — Potential Book Cover Concepts

1. **The Lantern in the Terminal** — A lone figure in a dark forest, holding a glowing lantern shaped like a command prompt cursor `>_`. The trees are made of cascading code. The light illuminates only the next few steps ahead.

2. **The Zen Garden with Circuit Paths** — A traditional Japanese rock garden, but the raked sand patterns are circuit board traces. A single stone sits at the center — engraved with the word `PROMPT`.

3. **The Craftsman's Bench** — An old wooden workbench covered in tools — but the tools are git commands, SPARQL queries, file editors. A half-built object sits in the center, glowing faintly. The craftsman's hands are human. The work is digital.

4. **The Bridge Builder** — Two cliff faces separated by a chasm. On one side: raw human thought, chaotic and organic. On the other: structured code, precise and ordered. A bridge of light connects them — mid-construction, not yet complete.

5. **The Two Moons** — A night sky with two moons. One is the familiar white moon — human intuition. The other is a darker, geometric moon — machine reasoning. A figure sits below, writing by the combined light of both.
