What counts as source
Source is whatever you have. Rarely just code.
Most projects bring four or five of these at once, and the weakest one sets the grade. The pile does not change between stage 0 and stage 1. What you ask of it does.
Context engineering, in practice
Cut the work until the context fits one window.
Too little
The model fills the gaps itself.
It invents your naming, your validation rules, your error handling, and it sounds sure about it.
The fit
One piece, and everything that piece needs.
One module. One group of microflows. One page and its bindings.
Too much
It loses the thread, and you pay for every token.
Detail from six other modules crowds out the part that matters, on every call after that.
The tree is not the point. The two marks on every leaf are the point. A piece is only a piece if what it needs fits, and if something other than the model can say it is done.

THE PIPELINE
A defined process
Nine stages, kickoff through cutover, in a fixed order. Each one reads the files the last one wrote.
Instructions for this project
Module names, conventions, what good looks like here. Written once, read at the start of every stage.
The wiring
One project folder. Sources, analysis, plans, scripts, reports. Every stage adds to it and nothing is thrown away.
Six agents, already defined
BA and architect decide, MDL drafts, gate, test and review check the result. None of them can change your model.
Skill packs
The Mendix knowledge the model does not have, carried as assets and not as prose.
Guarded writes
Check, snapshot, baseline, write, build gate. A regression restores the snapshot on its own.

Mendix Cloud
private cloud a preview URL
The pipeline pushes the build up through the Mendix platform APIs. Someone who knows the process opens the app and tries it.
What they say goes back in as requirements. The next run picks it up.
Headless does not mean unsupervised. It means the waiting happens without you. You review results, not progress.
Why generate into Mendix
Let’s call it “vibecoding”,
but without the governance & security risks.
The full stack, one model
Data, logic and UI are generated together as one model. There is no pile of glue code that nobody read on the way in.
Security lives in the model
Roles, entity access and page access are part of the model, and the platform enforces them at runtime. Nothing to audit line by line for the hole you missed.
Deployed like any other app
The run pushes the build up through the Mendix platform APIs, into a private cloud or your own on-premises environment. Same standards as everything else you run.
Still maintained tomorrow
What comes out is a supported platform app your team can keep building on, not a snapshot that starts aging the day it ships.
Generated fast, and it still lands where a real app has to land. Governed by the platform, deployed by the platform, supported tomorrow.
